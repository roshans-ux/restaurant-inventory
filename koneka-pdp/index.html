Amla & coconut

The Neelara ritual

  1. 1

    Warm

    Place 3–4 dropperfuls in a small bowl. Warm between palms — never microwave.

  2. 2

    Massage

    Work into scalp with fingertips in slow circles. Let gravity carry oil to mid-lengths.

  3. 3

    Rest

    Leave 45–60 minutes under a warm towel. Rinse with a gentle, sulphate-free cleanser.

Use twice weekly for visible support over 8–12 weeks.
